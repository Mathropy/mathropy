Hi there! I’m Hyeonki Son, also known as Mathropy. Born in 2002, I am a mathematics student from South Korea who creates original mathematical puzzles.

This blog is a dedicated space for the mathematical puzzles I create. I write them in English so that more people can explore them. And I am committed to sharing my journey here, as well as through GitHub, Substack, and Reddit.

**Why 'Mathropy'?**
My nickname is a blend of Math and Entropy. I've always been drawn to the word 'Entropy' for its sense of wild, unrestrained freedom. Interestingly, my favorite number is 7, and the word 'Entropy' happens to have exactly 7 letters with no repeating characters! By combining the two words, 'Math' and 'Entropy', I defined my own identity: 'Mathropy'! (Yes, It is 8 letters word. I know.)

**Join the Fun!**
Many of the puzzles I post are "open" in a sense—I don't always have the definitive answer or a formal proof yet. I invite you to tackle these problems not as a test, but as a fun logical exercise to ponder at your own pace.

I would love to hear your feedback on corrections, ideas for extensions, or even blog design suggestions. I’m always open to your messages, so don't hesitate to get in touch!
